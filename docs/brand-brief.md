# Brand Brief (`brand-brief.md`)

## 1. Brand Identity & Vibe
- **3 Descriptive Words:** Professional, Attrayant (Attractive), Jovial
- **Copywriting Tone:** Formal and bold. Direct, authoritative, yet inspiring and engaging.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Light Green (#4CAF50):** Represents growth, eco-efficiency, and successful budget control.
- **Turquoise Blue (#00BCD4):** Represents connectivity, clarity, technology, and data streams.
- **Warm Brown (#795548):** Represents grounding, reliability, and portfolio protection/stability.
- **Yellow-Mauve / Vibrant Accent (#9C27B0 / #FFEB3B):** Adds a touch of warmth, caution alerts, and visual contrast.

## 3. Typography & Visual Style
- **Primary Font:** "Maiandra GD" in heading/title hierarchy.
- **User Choice:** Support for 4 user-selectable font options in settings to personalize readability.
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Custom, bespoke UI design avoiding generic AI-generated templates. Clean cards, high-contrast indicators, and smooth data charts.
